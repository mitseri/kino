Biography

Rina Hidaka (日高 里菜, Hidaka Rina, born June 15, 1994) is a Japanese actress and voice actress from Chiba Prefecture. She is affiliated with StarCrew, having been previously with AT Production and Office Osawa. Announced the marriage on January 1, 2024, and her husband Yuma Uchida is also a voice actor.
