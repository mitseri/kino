Biography

Makoto Furukawa (古川 慎, Furukawa Makoto, born September 29, 1989) is a Japanese voice actor (seiyuu) and singer from Kumamoto Prefecture. He is a member of Toys Factory. He graduated from Fukuoka School of Music. In July 2018, he debuted as a singer under Lantis. On September 1 of the same year, he left Space Craft Entertainment, where he had been affiliated for six years, and joined Toys Factory on December 1 of the same year. In 2020, he won the Best Supporting Actor Award at the 14th Seiyū Awards.
