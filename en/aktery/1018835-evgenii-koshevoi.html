Biography

Yevhen Viktorovych Koshovyi is a Ukrainian showman, TV presenter, and comic actor. In the past, he was a member of the KVN team "Va-Bank" from Luhansk. Since 2005, he was the host of the show "Laugh Comic" and "Evening Neighbourhood". Since 2019, he was the host of the TV program "League of Laughter".
