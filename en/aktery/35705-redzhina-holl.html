Biography

Regina Lee Hall (born December 12, 1970) is an American actress. She rose to prominence for her role as Brenda Meeks in the comedy horror Scary Movie film series (2000–2026). She received critical acclaim and became the first African American to win the New York Film Critics Circle Award for Best Actress for the comedy film Support the Girls (2018).
