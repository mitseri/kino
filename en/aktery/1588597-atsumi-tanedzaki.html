Biography

Atsumi Tanezaki (種﨑敦美) is a Japanese voice actress born on September 27. She was formerly affiliated with Toritori Office, but is now represented by Haikyō. She has performed many well-known roles, including Anya Forger in SPY x FAMILY, and Frieren in Frieren: Beyond Journey's End.
